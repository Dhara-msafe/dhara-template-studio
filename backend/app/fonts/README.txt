DejaVu Sans (Regular, Bold) - bundled so PDF export can render characters
outside Latin-1 (the rupee sign, dashes, curly quotes) on any host.
Licence: DejaVu Fonts License (Bitstream Vera + Arev, free to bundle and
redistribute) - https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html
Note: DejaVu Sans has no Devanagari glyphs. Hindi/Marathi export (TS-11)
needs a Devanagari font plus fpdf2 text shaping; not handled yet.
